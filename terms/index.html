What Yardlancer Is

Yardlancer is a local marketplace connecting teens with gig and service opportunities in their own community. Yardlancer is not an employer, is not a party to any agreement between a gig poster and the person who completes it, and does not process payment for completed gigs — money for real-world work is handled directly between the two people involved, outside the app.

Who Can Use It

Any account under 13, or without a date of birth, must be linked to a parent or guardian account before using any feature beyond that linking flow itself. Posting a Premium-tier (higher-value) gig requires an 18-or-older account; claiming one does not. Accounts 13 through 17 can use standard features without a parent link.

Your Conduct

Be honest about the work you're offering or able to do. Show up when you say you will, or tell the other person as soon as you know you'll be late — this app tracks and reflects both. Don't harass, threaten, or attempt to contact someone who has blocked you.

Reporting and Blocking

Yardlancer has no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. That includes harassment, threats, hate, sexual content, anything that endangers or exploits another person (especially a minor), and trying to move a conversation with a minor off the app.

To report a post or comment, tap Report under it in the Square or a group chat. To block someone, tap Block under any of their posts or comments: their posts and comments disappear from the Square and group chats for you right away, and you can unblock them later in Settings, under Blocked Users. Blocking someone also sends us a notice so we can review their content.

We review every report and block notice, aiming to do so within 24 hours. Content that breaks these terms is removed, and the account that posted it may be suspended or permanently removed.

Content You Post

You keep ownership of what you post (listings, comments, reviews), but you're responsible for it, and you grant Yardlancer the right to display it to other users as the app's core function requires. Reviews reflect your honest assessment of a completed gig — reviews found to be dishonest or retaliatory may be removed.

Safety and Liability

Yardlancer facilitates introductions between people who then meet and work in person. We don't run full background checks on adult posters, do not supervise gigs, and cannot guarantee the safety, legality, or outcome of any gig arranged through the app. Use real judgment, especially for in-person meetings — the safety features in this app (ratings, location sharing during active gigs, parent linking for younger accounts) are meant to help with this, not replace it.

Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from Settings. This is immediate and permanent, and removes your account and the content tied to it.
