What happens next.

  1. Your gig appears on the map and someone nearby claims it.
  2. You both get a gig screen: they can flag they're running late or have arrived, and either of you can share location for that gig.
  3. When it's done, pay them directly, outside the app, and leave a review.

Good-neighbor guidelines.

  • Describe the job fully, since there are no direct messages.
  • Post from where the job is. The pin uses your phone's location.
  • Pay fairly. Be there when they arrive.
  • If a post or comment is a problem, use Report or Block.
