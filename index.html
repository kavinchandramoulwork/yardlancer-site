Real jobs. Real neighborhood.

Yardlancer connects teens with local work, one gig at a time.

Yard work, dog walking, tutoring, and more — matched with neighbors nearby. Built for teens looking to earn, with safety and parent-linking features for families along the way.

Hyperlocal gig discovery

A map-based view of real jobs nearby, not a generic feed. Browse what's actually available in your own neighborhood.

Two ways to earn

Quick Gigs ($15–35) cover the fast, everyday jobs — dog walking, yard cleanup, car washing, trash can valet, errands. Premium Services ($100–500) are bigger jobs like mobile detailing or window washing, posted by adult (18+) accounts.

Built for trust, not just transactions

Gig posters can rate each completed job, and ratings and a completed-gig count build up on a teen's profile, so reputation is earned over time — not just a name and a price.

A community, not just a marketplace

Group posts and a curated daily digest of nearby opportunities mean it's not just a static listings board — there's an actual local feed to check in on, not only a search you run when you need something.
