Here's exactly how it works, including what it doesn't do yet.

How linking works.

  1. Your kid gets a code in the app. It lasts 48 hours.
  2. You enter it in your own Yardlancer account.
  3. Before sharing starts, your kid sees a screen explaining it.

Required at 12 or under, or if your kid doesn't enter a birthday. Optional at 13 to 17.

Only share your linking code with your own parent or guardian.

Location while linked.

  • While linked, your kid's live location is shared with you whenever the app is open, once your kid has accepted the sharing screen and allowed location on their phone. No one else sees it.
  • During a gig, your kid can also choose to share location with the person they're working for, for that gig only.
  • A 13- to 17-year-old can end the link in Settings.

What we do, what we don't do.

What we do

  • Parent linking
  • Report and Block on every post and comment
  • Reports and block notices reviewed, aiming within 24 hours
  • No direct messages
  • Display names, not legal names

What we don't do

  • Full background checks on adult posters (not yet)
  • In-app payments
  • Supervising gigs
  • Confirming that whoever enters a linking code is the kid's parent. We check they're 18 or older, going by the birthday they entered.

Two things we suggest

  • Meet the neighbor first.
  • Go along for the first gig.

Questions.

Can my kid use it without me?

At 12 or under, or without a birthday on the account, no. The app stays locked until your kid's linking code is entered in another account. We check that the person entering it is 18 or older, going by the birthday they entered, but we can't confirm they're the parent, so your kid should only give the code to you. At 13 to 17, linking is optional.

Who can see the location?

While linked, you can see it whenever your kid's app is open, once your kid has accepted the sharing screen and allowed location on their phone. During a gig, your kid can also choose to share it with the person they're working for, for that gig only. No one else sees it.

Will I know if my teen unlinks?

There's no alert today. Your teen just leaves your Family screen.

Can strangers message my kid?

There are no direct messages. The Square is shared by everyone on Yardlancer, adults included, and group chats are split into under 18 and 18+, using the birthday entered at sign-up, which we don't check. Everything there is visible to the whole group, and every post and comment has Report and Block.

Who can post gigs?

Anyone with an account can post a Quick Gig. Posting a Premium gig needs an 18+ account, going by the birthday entered at sign-up, which we don't check. We don't run full background checks on adult posters yet. In some cases we may check an adult (18+) poster against public U.S. sex-offender registries by hand. Listings go live without review.

How does payment work?

Directly between the two people, outside the app. Yardlancer doesn't handle the money.

What if someone behaves badly?

Tap Report under a post or comment, or tap Block to hide that person's posts and comments right away. Blocking also sends us a notice, but it doesn't hide their gigs yet. We aim to review every report and block notice within 24 hours. For anything else, email support@yardlancer.com.
