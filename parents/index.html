Here's exactly how it works, including what it doesn't do yet.

How linking works.

  1. Your kid gets a code in the app. It lasts 48 hours.
  2. You enter it in your own Yardlancer account.
  3. Before sharing starts, your kid sees a screen explaining it.

Required at 12 or under, or if your kid doesn't enter a birthday. Optional at 13 to 17.

Only share your linking code with your own parent or guardian.

Location while linked.

  • While linked, your kid's live location is shared with you whenever the app is open. No one else sees it.
  • During a gig, your kid can also choose to share location with the person they're working for, for that gig only.
  • A 13- to 17-year-old can end the link in Settings.

What we do, what we don't do.

What we do

  • Parent linking
  • Report and Block on every post and comment
  • Reports and block notices reviewed, aiming within 24 hours
  • No direct messages
  • Display names, not legal names

What we don't do

  • Full background checks on adult posters (not yet)
  • In-app payments
  • Supervising gigs

Two things we suggest

  • Meet the neighbor first.
  • Go along for the first gig.

Questions.

Draft questions, to be replaced.

Can my kid use it without me?

At 12 or under, or without a birthday on the account, no. The app stays locked until a parent links. At 13 to 17, linking is optional.

Who can see the location?

While linked, you can, whenever your kid's app is open. No one else sees it. During a gig, your kid can also choose to share location with the person they're working for, for that gig only.

Will I know if my teen unlinks?

There's no alert today. Your teen just leaves your Family screen.

Can strangers message my kid?

There are no direct messages. The Square is shared by everyone, and group chats are split into under 18 and 18+. Everything there is visible to the whole group, and every post and comment has Report and Block.

Who can post gigs?

Anyone with an account can post a Quick Gig. Posting a Premium gig needs an 18+ account. We don't run full background checks on adult posters yet, and listings go live without review.

How does payment work?

Directly between the two people, outside the app. Yardlancer doesn't handle the money.

What if someone behaves badly?

Tap Report under a post or comment, or tap Block to hide that person's posts and comments right away. Blocking also sends us a notice. We aim to review every report and block notice within 24 hours. For anything else, email kavinchandramoul.work@gmail.com.

Be first on your street.

We'll email you when Yardlancer launches. That's all we use your address for.
